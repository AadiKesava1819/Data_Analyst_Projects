# HR Analytics Dashboard 

This project is an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built to provide insights into employee attrition within a company. It presents data in a visual format for easier understanding of trends and patterns related to employee turnover.

##  Features

- **Total Employees**: Displays the total headcount.
- **Attrition Metrics**: Shows attrition count, attrition rate, and breakdown by various factors.
- **Demographic Insights**:
  - Average Age and Salary
  - Years at Company
- **Attrition Breakdown**:
  - By Gender
  - By Education Level
  - By Salary Range
  - By Age Group
  - By Job Role
- **Job Satisfaction**: Attrition correlated with job satisfaction across roles.

##  Visual Insights

- **Pie Charts**: Education-wise attrition
- **Bar Charts**: Age group, salary range, and job role comparisons
- **Line Graph**: Attrition by individual age
- **Matrix**: Job Satisfaction vs. Job Role

##  Departments Covered

- Human Resources
- Research & Development
- Sales

##  Technologies Used

- Power BI for data visualization
- PNG export of the dashboard for sharing

##  Insights

- The **26-35 age group** sees the highest attrition.
- Most attrition comes from **Life Sciences and Medical backgrounds**.
- **Low salary ranges (up to 5k)** contribute to the highest attrition.
- **Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives** are the top roles for attrition.
